## Algorithms & Academic References

- **SuperMemo-2 (SM-2)**: Spaced repetition scheduling algorithm by Piotr Woźniak.
  - Woźniak, P. A. (1990). *Optimization of Learning*. University of Opole.
- **HippoRAG**: Associative memory retrieval concept inspired by hippocampal indexing theory.
- **Karpathy LLM-Wiki**: Knowledge compilation architecture inspired by Andrej Karpathy's writings on LLM-augmented knowledge bases.
- **Kahneman Dual-Process Theory**: Cognitive architecture inspired by Daniel Kahneman's *Thinking, Fast and Slow* (2011).
