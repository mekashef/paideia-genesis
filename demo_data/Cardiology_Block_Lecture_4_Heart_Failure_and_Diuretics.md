## 1. Hemodynamics of Heart Failure
Left ventricular failure elevates Left Ventricular End-Diastolic Pressure (LVEDP), which transmits retrograde into Left Atrial Pressure (LAP) and Pulmonary Capillary Wedge Pressure (PCWP).
When PCWP exceeds 18–20 mmHg, Starling forces favor fluid transudation into the lung interstitium and alveoli -> **Acute Pulmonary Edema**.

### Symptoms:
- Paroxysmal Nocturnal Dyspnea (PND)
- Orthopnea (quantified by number of pillows)
- Inspiratory crackles / rales, bilateral S3 gallop

## 2. Diuretic Therapeutics in Volume Overload
### Loop Diuretics:
- **Agents**: Furosemide, Bumetanide, Torsemide, Ethacrynic Acid.
- **Site & Mechanism**: Thick Ascending Limb of the Loop of Henle. Competitively blocks the apical **Na+/K+/2Cl- (NKCC2)** cotransporter.
- **Electrolyte Consequences**:
  - Increased excretion of Na+, Cl-, K+, Mg2+, Ca2+, and H+.
  - Promotes hypokalemic, hypochloremic metabolic alkalosis.
  - "Loops Lose Calcium" (unlike thiazides, which retain calcium).
- **Toxicities**: Ototoxicity (reversible, dose-dependent, synergistic with aminoglycosides), Hypokalemia, Hyperuricemia / Gout, Sulfa allergy (Ethacrynic acid is safe in sulfa allergy).

## 3. Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy (GDMT) - Chronic vs Acute
### The 4 Pillars of Chronic HFrEF (Mortality Benefit):
1. **ARNI / ACEi / ARB**: Inhibit maladaptive remodeling driven by Angiotensin II.
2. **Beta-Blockers (Carvedilol, Metoprolol Succinate, Bisoprolol)**: Reduce chronic sympathetic neurohormonal toxicity and arrhythmias.
3. **Mineralocorticoid Receptor Antagonists (Spironolactone, Eplerenone)**: Blunts aldosterone-mediated cardiac fibrosis.
4. **SGLT2 Inhibitors (Dapagliflozin, Empagliflozin)**: Reduces hospitalizations and cardiovascular mortality.

### CRITICAL BOARD EXAM WARNING:
> **Do NOT initiate or up-titrate Beta-Blockers in Acute Decompensated Heart Failure (ADHF)!**  
> While beta-blockers reduce mortality in *stable ambulatory* patients, acute administration causes acute negative inotropy that unmasks cardiac fatigue and precipitates **cardiogenic shock**. Only continue low-dose if the patient was already chronically stabilized on it; never initiate during active pulmonary edema.
